# Clinical Registry Protocol Notes

These notes guide research-field design only. They do not provide patient-specific diagnostic or treatment advice.

| Area | Research-field implication | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CIDP | Capture structured clinical phenotype and nerve-conduction / EMG-NCS evidence, with supportive investigations such as CSF and imaging or laboratory exclusion work-up recorded as applicable rather than mandatory for every case. | [EAN/PNS CIDP guideline](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34327760/) |
| MS functional assessment | Record the MS Functional Composite (MSFC) as structured component values for ambulation, upper-limb function, and cognitive function rather than an unvalidated single score. | [Fischer et al., PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10467383/) |
| Stroke risk profile | Add HbA1c and fasting lipid profile as structured research-relevant risk-factor investigations; uric acid is retained because the requested local research protocol explicitly requires it. | [AHA/ASA secondary prevention guideline](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/10.1161/STR.0000000000000375) |
| Infection exclusion before immune therapy | Record a cohort- and therapy-relevant protocol review, not a one-size-fits-all prescribing checklist. The registry captures a status and research-safe note for TB, hepatitis, HIV, VZV, or other infection-exclusion review as locally appropriate, while the treating team determines individual investigations and referral. | [EULAR chronic/opportunistic infection guidance](https://ard.bmj.com/content/annrheumdis/early/2022/11/17/ard-2022-223335.full.pdf); [MS infection screening review](https://academic.oup.com/ofid/article/5/8/ofy174/5054278) |
