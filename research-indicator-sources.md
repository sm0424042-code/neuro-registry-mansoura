# Aggregate Neurology Indicator Sources

This working source log supports only aggregate, research-oriented indicator definitions in the protected Statistics workspace. It does not contain patient data, record data, personal identifiers, or clinical narratives.

| Cohort | Source | Aggregate indicator implication |
|---|---|---|
| Stroke | American Heart Association, [Get With The Guidelines®–Stroke Recognition Criteria](https://www.heart.org/en/professional/quality-improvement/get-with-the-guidelines/get-with-the-guidelines-stroke/get-with-the-guidelines-stroke-recognition-criteria) | The programme identifies IV thrombolysis performance for eligible acute ischaemic stroke presentations. The registry can report its existing controlled reperfusion category as a cohort-level proportion, but does not infer eligibility, treatment timeliness, or outcome unless those controlled fields exist. |
| Multiple Sclerosis | American Academy of Neurology, [Multiple Sclerosis Quality Measures](https://www.aan.com/practice/multiple-sclerosis-quality-measures) | The AAN lists MRI monitoring and disease-modifying therapy monitoring among MS quality measures. The registry can support controlled aggregate DMT, disease-course, relapse-activity, and MSFC-completion indicators; it must not claim the broader measure specifications when the required fields are absent. |
| Guillain–Barré Syndrome | European Academy of Neurology / Peripheral Nerve Society, [Guideline on diagnosis and treatment of Guillain–Barré syndrome](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jns.12594) | The guideline covers diagnosis and treatment of GBS. The registry can support aggregate variants, treatment category, disability score, and ventilatory-support indicators drawn from its controlled fields, without inferring prognosis or treatment appropriateness. |
| Myasthenia Gravis | Myasthenia Gravis Foundation of America, [Resources for Professionals](https://myasthenia.org/professionals/resources-for-professionals/) | MGFA identifies clinical classification and therapy status as research-relevant descriptive tools. The registry can support controlled aggregate MGFA class, antibody status, thymoma status, crisis history, and treatment-response measures, without interpreting comparative effectiveness. |
| CIDP | European Academy of Neurology / Peripheral Nerve Society, [Guideline on diagnosis and treatment of CIDP](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/ene.14959) | The guideline covers diagnostic category, CIDP variants, and treatment. The registry can support aggregate variant, diagnostic-pathway, electrophysiology, CSF-protein, and disability-level indicators from controlled values; it does not determine diagnostic validity or therapy effect. |
| Abnormal Movements | American Academy of Neurology, [Movement Disorders Quality Measures](https://www.aan.com/practice/movement-disorders-quality-measures) | AAN quality measures include structured assessment themes for Parkinson disease and essential tremor. The registry can support aggregate movement phenotype, distribution, severity, functional impact, and treatment-response indicators from its controlled fields; it does not infer diagnosis subtype or quality of care not represented in the data. |
| Neuro-ophthalmology / NMOSD | European Academy of Neurology, [Update on diagnosis and treatment of NMOSD](https://www.ean.org/research/resources/neurology-updates/detail/update-on-the-diagnosis-and-treatment-of-neuromyelitis-optica-spectrum-disorders-nmosd) | The update distinguishes AQP4-IgG-positive NMOSD and describes AQP4-IgG as a diagnostic marker. The registry can report AQP4-positive (and both-positive) results among controlled NMOSD-classified entries; it cannot confirm diagnosis or treatment eligibility. |
| Myelopathy | American College of Radiology, [Appropriateness Criteria: Myelopathy](https://acsearch.acr.org/docs/69484/Narrative/) | The criteria distinguish acute, chronic/progressive, and vascular-feature myelopathy imaging scenarios. The registry can support aggregate anatomical level, controlled cause, upper/lower motor-neuron feature, bladder involvement, and protocol-imaging completion indicators, without asserting imaging appropriateness for an individual. |

## Design rule

Every proposed measure must map to existing, controlled registry values, report aggregate numerator and denominator only, enforce the approved-user server gate, and avoid causal or clinical-effect claims. Measures with a small denominator will be suppressed before display or export.
